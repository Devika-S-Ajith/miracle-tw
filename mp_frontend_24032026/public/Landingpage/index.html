Helping Children Thrive Across the Globe

ThriveWell is currently being used with foster care and prevention partners across the United States and across the globe in India and Africa to help ensure children receive the support and resources they need to thrive in families.

What Can It Do?

ThriveWell is much more than a logging app. It’s really your complete digital assistant that connects you to the entire child welfare ecosystem.

Easy Logging

Easily keep families updated with required logging and proactively reach out to those falling behind through push notifications.

Shared Calendar

Add important deadlines, trainings, and events, all with RSVP ability & push notification reminders.

Data-Driven Decisions

Collect the data you need to understand your cases to connect them to the resources they need most when they need it.

AI Analysis

ThriveWell not only collects data but also uses AI technology to analyze notes, helping case managers prioritize their cases.

Integrated Interventions

ThriveWell creates a practical action plan, tailors interventions and continuous follow-up reports help ensure families thrive.

Flexible Assessments

With fully customizable questions, our assessment tool can be used to serve any population across a variety of different use cases.

Saving Time For What Matters

Forget the long complex reporting, just a few daily clicks and you’re done. Case managers are alerted to negative trends through the app without wading through paperwork.

Never Miss An Important Date Again

Use our built-in calendar to sync important dates between social workers and caregivers so everyone is always on the same page.
