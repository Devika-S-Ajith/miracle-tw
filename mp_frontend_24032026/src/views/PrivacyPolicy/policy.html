ThriveWell – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2024


  1. Introduction

    1. This Privacy Policy (the “Privacy Policy”) describes Miracle Foundation, Inc.’s (“Miracle Foundation,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collection, use, disclosure, and other processing of personal information that you provide to us or that we collect via the access to and use of our website, http://www.thrivewellapp.com, which includes text, media, documentation, pictures and other content on this ThriveWell website and the associated services (collectively, the “Website”), or the mobile application and software provided by Miracle Foundation as part of the ThriveWell platform (the “App”). Any reference to the Website in this Privacy Policy shall also be deemed to include the App.


    2. IMPORTANT NOTE: By accessing or using the Website or App you agree to Miracle Foundation’s collection, use, and disclosure of your or your child’s personal information as described in this Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy is incorporated into and subject to the Terms of Use for the Website and App (“Terms of Use”), a copy of which is available at http://www.thrivewellapp.com/terms. People who visit the Website or use the App are referred to as “Users.”


    3. [IMPORTANT NOTE: We also obtain a separate consent form within the App from all Users of the App.]


II. Restrictions On Use

  1. International Users. Our Website is not intended for use outside of the United States, India, and Uganda. If you are a resident of another country, your use of the Website may be governed by laws that are less protective than those of your home jurisdiction. You are responsible for monitoring and complying with the laws of the jurisdiction where you are a resident or are using the Website or the App.

  2. Children’s Privacy. Users must be at least 18 years old to create an account or register for the Website or App. We do not collect personally identifiable data from any person we know to be under the age of 13 without consent of such child’s parent, legal guardian, or the agency or individual granted legal conservatorship of the child, and we instruct children under 13 not to send us any information unless we have obtained such consent. [Miracle Foundation has implemented policies and procedures to ensure the integrity and security of the collection and processing of personal data of children.] Miracle Foundation does not knowingly ask for any personal information from children under the age of 13 without first obtaining the consent of such child’s parent, legal guardian, or the agency or individual legally granted conservatorship of the child. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 years old in a way that is inconsistent with the “applicable child safety laws”, then Miracle Foundation will use commercially reasonable efforts to promptly delete this information.


III. Information that Miracle Foundation Collects From You

  • Miracle Foundation may collect personal information from you, such as your or your child’s first and last name, location, address, state and county of residence, email address, photo, username and password, phone number, employer, and any other information you provide to us when you create an account to log into our network or use our Website (your “Account”).


  • If you use our App on your mobile device, we may collect your phone number, mobile software and hardware information, and unique device ID number.


  • If you provide us feedback or contact us via email, we will collect your name and email address, as well as any other content included in the email, to assist in sending you a reply.


  • Miracle Foundation may collect information that Users submit to the Website, such as comments, uploaded media, postings, messages, text, files, images, graphics, photos, audio clips, sounds, video, or other materials, or messages to other Users, including such materials that may contain personally-identifiable information.


  • Miracle Foundation also collects other information voluntarily provided by Users to Miracle Foundation via email or via any sign-up or opt-in pages of the Website.


IV. Information Collected via Technology

  1. Information Collected by our Website. To make our Website more useful to you, we automatically track certain information about the visits to our Website, including your browser type, operating system, Internet Protocol (IP) address, clickstream data, referring and exiting pages, and a date/time stamp for your visit. Miracle Foundation uses cookies, web beacons, log file information, and similar mechanisms for all or any of the following purposes:


    1. To store information so that you will not have to re-enter it during your visit or the next time you visit the Website.


    2. To deliver custom, personalized content and information.


    3. Identify you when you access, visit, or log-in to the Website.


    4. To note the different areas of the Website that have recently been accessed through your computer. Information collected in this way may be used to develop and manage the online services of Miracle Foundation by, for example, storing information about your preferences so as to enable Miracle Foundation to customize the Website according to your individual interests.


    5. Improving the content of the Website and conducting various other diagnostics to improve the Website. Miracle Foundation may, for example, use cookies to monitor aggregate metrics such as total number of User and pages viewed.


    6. Analyzing and improving Website security.


  2. Cookies. Like many online services, we use cookies to collect information. “Cookies” are small pieces of information that a website sends to your computer’s hard drive while you are viewing the website. We may use both session Cookies (which expire once you close your web browser) and persistent Cookies (which stay on your computer until you delete them) to provide you with a more personal and interactive experience on our Website. This type of information is collected to make the Website more useful to you and to tailor your experience to meet your special interests.


  3. Mobile Services. We may also collect non-personal information from your mobile device if you have downloaded our App. This information is generally used to help us deliver the most relevant information to you. Examples of information that may be collected and used include how you use the App, and information about the type of device you use. In addition, in the event our App crashes on your mobile device, we will receive information about your mobile device model software version and wireless service carrier, which allows us to identify and fix bugs and otherwise improve the performance of our App.


  4. Third-Party Analytics. We use certain third-party service providers, such as Google Analytics, to help analyze how users use our Website (“Analytics Companies”). These Analytics Companies use certain technologies, like Cookies, to collect information such as how often users visit our Website, what pages they visit, where they go, and what other sites they used prior to coming to our Website. We use the information we get from Analytics Companies to improve our Website, or to develop new product offerings.


  5. Location Information. We use location-related information such as your current location and where you live to personalize and improve our Website for you and others. Location-related information can be based on things like precise device location (if you have allowed us to collect it), IP addresses, and information from your and others’ use of our Website.

VII. How We Use Information

  1. General Use. In general, the personal information you submit to us is used to operate the Website, to respond to the requests you make, or to aid us in serving you better. [Your data will also be used for enhancing our services through profiling and automated decision-making processes, which help us personalize and improve your user experience.] We use your personal information in the following ways:


    1. to facilitate the creation of and secure your Account on our network


    2. to identify you as a user in our system and to otherwise administer and operate our Website


    3. to facilitate your communications with other Users


    4. to improve the quality of experience when you interact with our Website


    5. to send you administrative email notifications, such as security or support and maintenance advisories


    6. to deliver customized or personalized content and information


    7. to respond to your inquiries and other requests


  2. Feedback. We may use your feedback to enhance or modify the Website, App or services.

  3. Creation of Anonymous Data. We may create anonymized data (data that is not reasonably associated with or linked to any data that would reasonably be expected to allow someone to identify you or contact you) from personal information by excluding information (such as your name) that makes the data personally identifiable to you. We use this anonymized data to analyze requests and usage patterns so that we may enhance the content of our Website and App and improve Website and App navigation. We reserve the right to use anonymized data for any purpose and disclose anonymized data to third parties.

When you use the Website, the personal information you provide will be shared with other Users that you designate to receive such information, such as case managers, caregivers, single source continuum contractors, child care institutions, child placement agencies, or foster care agencies.
